Why This Property Sits at the Top of Eagle Creek's Rental Stock

Eagle Creek is not one market — it is roughly 2,600 residences across 18 villages, built in phases over two decades behind a 24-hour guard gate. The community's early villages (circa 2005–2012) consist largely of smaller-format homes on compact homesites, some as small as 0.13 acres. The later phases are a different product generation entirely: when the community's developer — the Emerson Group, an international real estate group building since 1959, through its Jones Homes USA subsidiary — opened remaining land to additional premium builders, the resulting wave of executive product elevated Eagle Creek well beyond a typical production golf community.

10822 Mobberley Circle belongs to that later executive generation, and it carries a combination of attributes that almost never appears together in the community's rental inventory:

  • Private screened pool AND spa — most Eagle Creek residents rely on the community's resort pools; a private pool is uncommon, and a private pool with spa in a rental is genuinely rare. The pool is recent construction with one of the finest sightlines in the community.
  • Dual premium exposure — the property backs to both the 4.5-star championship golf course and water. Golf-front or pond-view homes each command premiums; this home has both.
  • Executive scale — 4,817 heated square feet with formal and informal living spaces, bonus/game room, and a primary suite with custom walk-in closet, in a community where the typical single-family rental runs 2,000–3,200 square feet.
  • 3-car garage with Tesla charging connection — a differentiator that speaks directly to this corridor's executive and physician tenant profile.
  • Full-service lawn and pool & spa care included — the residents enjoy the estate; the owner's landscaping and pool systems stay professionally protected. At this property's scale, this inclusion carries real monthly value of roughly $400–$500.

The community's proximity to Medical City makes it a standing favorite of relocating physicians and executives — exactly the tenant pool for a home of this caliber, and a pool that renews reliably and treats a property of this quality with the care it deserves.

The Comparable Landscape — And Why Most of It Doesn't Apply

An honest analysis at this tier begins by discarding what doesn't compare. Eagle Creek's mid-tier rental stock — solid homes of 2,500–3,200 square feet — trades at a meaningfully lower rate per square foot than executive product. Applying that arithmetic to an executive estate would be a category error: the tiers serve different tenants at different price-per-foot levels, which is why we have excluded that inventory entirely rather than let it drag the analysis downward.

The Scarcity That Sets the Price

Renters can find square footage in Lake Nona. What they essentially cannot find is a guard-gated, golf-and-water-front executive home with a private pool and spa, offered unfurnished for genuine long-term living, with the estate's grounds and pool professionally maintained as part of the rent. That is a one-of-one listing in this community at this moment — and one-of-one is precisely when a premium price is defensible.
